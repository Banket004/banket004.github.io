How to Play

  1. 1 The secret number consists of non-repeating digits
  2. 2 Enter your guess using the number keyboard
  3. 3 Results show AxB format:
    A = Correct digit in correct position
    B = Correct digit but wrong position
  4. 4 Example: "1234 = 2A1B" means 2 digits are correct in the right position, 1 digit is correct but in the wrong position
  5. 5 Win by getting 4A0B (Beginner), 5A0B (Intermediate), or 6A0B (Ultimate)!
